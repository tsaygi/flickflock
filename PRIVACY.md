# FlickFlock Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 25 November 2026_

FlickFlock is an image viewer that runs entirely on your Mac.

**FlickFlock does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data.** It has no accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking or network features, and it does not contact any server.

What stays on your Mac:

- **Your images** are only read to display them. Edits are written only when you save.
- **Settings** (key bindings, preferences) are stored in the app's own folder on your Mac.
- **Thumbnails** are cached on your Mac to make browsing faster, and can be cleared under Settings > Performance.
- **Folder access:** when you allow FlickFlock to open a folder, macOS gives it a bookmark to that folder so it does not have to ask again. The bookmark stays on your Mac.

Removing FlickFlock removes this data along with the app.

FlickFlock is open source; you can check this at https://github.com/tsaygi/flickflock.

Questions: please open an issue at https://github.com/tsaygi/flickflock/issues.
